AUTO AIR STOP FILTER PRIMING FILTER
Specially designed Auto Air Stop filter
prevents the entry of air in line when
the I.V. bottle is empty
Auto Air Stop filter maintains a
constant fluid level and reduces the
possibility of air in the I.V. Line
The special Priming Filter ensures that the
line fills automatically (Auto Prime) thus
maintains a closed system
Helps in reducing Healthcare Associated
Infections (HAI)
Reduces spillage and wastage of I.V. fluid
