Each medical refrigerator/ Freezer should have a TMD (Temperature Monitoring Device).
Accurate temperature records can show the actual temperature of vaccines, drugs, or
samples, which is essential for the protection of the contents inside the unit. It is less costly
to purchase a reliable device than to replace the vaccines that are wasted due to loss of
potency – caused by exposure to out-of-range temperatures.
