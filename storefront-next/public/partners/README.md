# Official partner logos

1. Add approved image assets here (PNG, SVG, or WebP).
2. Register each item in `lib/partners.ts` with its actual partner name and `/partners/<filename>` image path.
3. Only use logos you have permission to display. Do not rename or redraw the partner's mark without approval.
4. Check logo legibility against white backgrounds. Transparent PNG or SVG is ideal.
5. With no registered logos, the section shows clearly labeled example logo slots in development and is hidden in a production build. Once approved logos are registered, an animated logo carousel replaces the placeholders.

The carousel is located directly beneath the product categories in the Home page's **Explore Our Offerings** section.
