About Iraseth Pharma

Who we are

Iraseth Pharma Incorporated is a Philippine-based company providing integrated medical and diagnostics solutions for the country's foremost hospitals and clinical laboratories. Ever since its establishment in July 2013, we've been one of the country's rapidly growing trading companies in healthcare solutions — importing, indenting, and distributing medical and diagnostic devices, laboratory supplies, and consumables at cost, for local government units and private facilities alike.

We take pride in contributing to society by creating a safe working environment for every patient and the entire healthcare team, backed by ISO 9001-aligned quality standards.

What drives us

Mission, vision & commitment

Mission

To provide access to quality pharmaceutical and medical device products with innovative and safety features that adhere to high standards of quality and service — creating a safe working environment in hospital settings and communities.

Vision

We envision ourselves as a prime provider of high-quality medical devices and pharmaceutical products that substantially upgrade the quality of health services for the Filipino people — and to eradicate needle stick injuries in hospital settings while promoting early screening for diabetes and communicable diseases.

Commitment

To make a difference by being a responsible organization that builds and supports a safe working environment for every healthcare provider, patient, and community.
